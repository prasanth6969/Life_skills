# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit



Angela Duckworth says talent and IQ are not the main reason people succeed. What matters more is grit, which means staying passionate and not giving up on a long-term goal for years. She says success is like a marathon, not a sprint, and we can build grit by believing our ability can grow with effort.

---

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset


A fixed mindset means thinking our abilities are fixed, so we avoid challenges and fear failing. A growth mindset means believing we can improve with effort, good strategies and feedback, so mistakes become a way to learn. This matters because it helps us learn and perform better in everything we do.

---

## 3. What is Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video ?

- Internal locus of control means believing that my own actions, effort and choices decide what happens to me, not luck, fate or other people.
- The key point is that people who feel in control of their results stay more motivated. They focus on what they can change and take responsibility instead of making excuses. So to stay motivated, I should focus on my own actions.

---

## 4. How to Build a Growth Mindset



1. Believe in your ability to figure things out
2. Question your assumptions
3. Create your own curriculum for growth
4. Honor the struggle

---



## 5. What are my ideas to take action and build a Growth Mindset ?


- Treat bugs and errors as feedback, not as proof that I am bad at coding. I will try to debug first, then ask for help with specific doubts.
- Write down what I learned from each mistake or challenge.
- Ask for code reviews and use the feedback without getting defensive.
- Focus on what I can control, like my effort, practice time and the way I learn.
- Celebrate my effort and progress, not only the final result.
- Stay consistent every day, even when it feels hard.
