6. Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do.
- In simple words, if you want to start a new habit, the things related to that habit should be kept on your desk or in your workspace so that it is easier to do.

7. Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do.
- As per the book's perspective, increase friction to make the behavior more difficult or undesirable. The goal is to design your environment to place as many barriers as possible between you and the bad habit.
