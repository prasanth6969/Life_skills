# Prevention of Sexual Harassment 

## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

- Unwanted touching, grabbing, hugging, or blocking someone's way.
- Sexual jokes, comments, or remarks about someone's body or looks.
- Staring, whistling, or making suggestive gestures.
- Sending sexual messages, emails, or pictures, or asking for dates again and again.
- Treating someone unfairly because of their gender, such as giving extra work or blocking promotion.

## 2. What would I do if I face or witness such behaviour?

- First, I will tell the person to stop because I don't like it.
- I will note down what happened, like the date, time, place and who was there.
- If it happens again, I will write a mail to  higher authorities like HR or Manager.
- If I see it happening to someone else, I will not ignore it. I will tell the higher authorities and i'll help that person.
- If it is very serious, I will go to the police.
